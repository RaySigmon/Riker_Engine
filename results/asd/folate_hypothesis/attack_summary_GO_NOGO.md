## The Honest Picture

### What's STRONG (survived all attacks cleanly):
1. **The immune signature is real, large, and ASD-specific** — 59 genes, 95% upregulated, large effect sizes (>0.7 for 15+ genes), unique composition not seen in AD/BC/CRC/psoriasis, and the pattern (complement + IFN + antigen presentation) is specifically consistent with autoantibody-driven pathology, not generic inflammation
2. **The FRAA → CFD → ASD pathway is independently well-supported** — Frye's 71% prevalence, positive leucovorin trial (d=0.91), FOLR1 genetic evidence
3. **MAT2B and Cluster 21 are ASD-specific** — not found in any other disease in the Riker Engine
4. **The alternative explanations (oxidative stress, generic neuroinflammation, primary mito disease) all failed** — the immune signal is too specific and the mito signal is too regulatory to be explained by simpler mechanisms

### What's WEAK (survived with serious caveats):
1. **The UMFA → FRAA link is the biggest gap** — plausible but zero experimental evidence. This is the hinge. The hypothesis stands or falls on this arrow.
2. **3 million participants say FA is protective** — the subpopulation argument (rare adverse pathway in susceptible subset) is necessary but currently ad hoc
3. **The metabolic/folate-specific data is thin** — MAT2B tiny effect, ENTPD6 confused, several mito genes near-zero. The folate connection is inferential, not direct.
4. **The CDR framing is overreach** — CDR-adjacent signal (4/9 categories) but not a coherent CDR program. Dropping "CDR" from the label and calling it "folate deficiency → neuroinflammation → aberrant pruning" would be more defensible.
5. **The HR 17.6 B12 synergy is unreplicated** — elegant mechanistic model built on a fragile statistic from a small cohort

### What CHANGED because of the attacks:
- The CDR label should be **demoted** from central to supporting
- The immune/autoimmune arm should be **promoted** to the lead story
- The metabolic arm (MAT2B, Cluster 21, mito genes) is **supporting evidence**, not the headline
- The B12 peripheral sink model should be presented as **hypothesis-generating**, not confirmed
- The UMFA → FRAA gap must be **flagged prominently**, not buried
- The paper needs to explicitly address the protective epidemiological evidence and define the subpopulation argument precisely

## GO / NO-GO DECISION

### GO — With mandatory reframing.

**Rationale:** All 5 attacks failed to kill the core hypothesis. The immune signature is independently publishable and scientifically interesting. The FRAA → CFD → ASD chain is well-supported by existing literature. The Riker Engine cross-reference provides novel computational evidence (MAT2B, immune signature, disease specificity).

**But the paper MUST:**

1. **Lead with what's strong** — the ASD-specific neuroinflammatory transcriptomic signature discovered by the Riker Engine, its consistency with FRAA-driven autoimmune pathology
2. **Present the UMFA → FRAA → CFD chain as a hypothesis to test**, not a demonstrated mechanism
3. **Address the 3M-participant protective evidence head-on** — frame as: "FA is net protective at the population level, but the U-shaped curve (Raghavan 2018) and 71% FRAA prevalence in ASD suggest a susceptibility subset where dose matters"
4. **Downgrade CDR to a supporting framework**, not the central claim. "Consistent with CDR" is defensible. "This is CDR" is not.
5. **Present B12 synergy as hypothesis-generating**, explicitly noting it's unreplicated
6. **Be scrupulously honest about effect sizes** — MAT2B is real but small, ENTPD6 is directionally uncertain, SDHA/NDUFAF5 have near-zero core fold changes
