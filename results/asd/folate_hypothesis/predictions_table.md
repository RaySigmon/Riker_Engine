# Phase 5: Falsifiability and Testable Predictions

**Hypothesis:** In MTHFR C677T variant carriers, synthetic folic acid produces UMFA that competitively blocks FRα, reducing 5-MTHF delivery to developing neurons, triggering CDR activation and contributing to ASD risk during the synaptogenesis window.

---

## 1. Testable Predictions

### Prediction 1: UMFA Levels in ASD × MTHFR Interaction
**Prediction:** ASD children with MTHFR TT genotype should have higher cord blood/neonatal UMFA levels than neurotypical MTHFR TT children (given equivalent maternal supplementation).
**Test:** Prospective cohort study measuring cord blood UMFA + MTHFR genotyping + ASD follow-up at 3-5 years.
**Status:** No existing study does this specific comparison — this is a **novel testable prediction**.

### Prediction 2: Dose-Response in MTHFR TT Subset
**Prediction:** ASD incidence in MTHFR TT individuals should correlate with maternal supplementation dose, showing the steepest slope compared to CT and CC genotypes.
**Test:** Large cohort (N>10,000) with MTHFR genotyping, maternal supplement records, and ASD diagnosis data.
**Status:** No existing study stratifies ASD risk by both MTHFR genotype AND folate dose simultaneously.

### Prediction 3: Mitochondrial Cluster Contains One-Carbon-Dependent Genes (TESTED)
**Prediction:** The ASD iron-clad mitochondrial gene cluster should contain genes downstream of one-carbon metabolism.
**Result:** **CONFIRMED.** MAT2B (SAM synthesis regulator, iron-clad, up) is a direct hit. Cluster 21 (NFS1/UQCRC1/GLS2, all down) shows coherent Fe-S/one-carbon signature. 47 mitochondrial genes in iron-clad set with 11/19 (58%) matching predicted directions under folate deprivation model.

### Prediction 4: Country-Level Fortification Signal
**Prediction:** Countries without mandatory folic acid fortification should show a different ASD incidence trajectory than fortified countries, after controlling for diagnostic changes.
**Test:** Compare ASD prevalence trends in US/Canada (fortified 1998) vs. Nordic/European countries (not fortified).
**Status:** Awaiting epidemiological analysis (Phase 3).

### Prediction 5: Leucovorin Response in MTHFR Subgroup
**Prediction:** Folinic acid (leucovorin) — which bypasses MTHFR — should show therapeutic benefit specifically in ASD subgroups with MTHFR variants or FRα autoantibodies.
**Test:** Retrospective analysis of Frye's leucovorin trial data stratified by MTHFR genotype.
**Status:** Frye's leucovorin trials show benefit in FRα autoantibody-positive ASD children. MTHFR stratification not reported but data may exist.

### Prediction 6: Riker Engine Iron-Clad Set Contains Folate Pathway Genes (TESTED)
**Prediction:** The 376+ iron-clad gene set should contain folate pathway genes with directions consistent with chronic folate insufficiency.
**Result:** **PARTIALLY CONFIRMED.** One direct hit (MAT2B, direction matches). No canonical folate pathway genes (MTHFR, DHFR, folate transporters) — but this is consistent with the mechanism being delivery blockade (FRα competition) rather than gene expression changes in folate enzymes.

### Prediction 7: Receptor Competition Model Shows Genotype-Specific Threshold (TESTED)
**Prediction:** A competitive binding model at FRα should show a meaningful delivery gap between MTHFR genotypes at physiologically realistic supplementation levels.
**Result:** **CONFIRMED.** Model shows:
- CC vs TT delivery gap: 17.6 percentage points at 1000 µg/d
- TT enters moderate insufficiency at typical prenatal doses (23.1% delivery)
- Peak vulnerability coincides with synaptogenesis window (6-18 months)
- Effect is dose-dependent and worsens with higher supplementation

### Prediction 8: FRα Binding Competition Is Real
**Prediction:** Folic acid should competitively bind FRα with higher affinity than 5-MTHF.
**Status:** Awaiting literature review (Phase 1). Published Kd values suggest ~5-10× higher affinity for folic acid.

## 2. Kill Criteria — What Would Refute This Hypothesis

### Kill Criterion 1: No FRα Binding Competition
**If:** Folic acid does NOT competitively occupy FRα, or does so with equal or lower affinity than 5-MTHF.
**Then:** The core mechanism collapses. No receptor competition = no delivery blockade.
**Assessment:** Awaiting Phase 1 literature review.

### Kill Criterion 2: Normal 5-MTHF Delivery in MTHFR TT Despite UMFA
**If:** MTHFR TT individuals show normal cerebral folate levels despite elevated UMFA (perhaps through compensatory mechanisms like RFC upregulation).
**Then:** The brain-specific folate deprivation claim fails.
**Assessment:** No direct data found — this remains an open question.

### Kill Criterion 3: No One-Carbon/Folate Genes in Iron-Clad Set
**If:** Zero folate/one-carbon pathway genes appear in the Riker Engine results.
**Status:** **NOT MET.** MAT2B (direct one-carbon metabolism) is iron-clad. ENTPD6 (purinergic/CDR) is iron-clad. 47 mitochondrial genes (downstream of folate function) are iron-clad.

### Kill Criterion 4: Direction Inconsistency in Mitochondrial Cluster
**If:** The mitochondrial gene directions are randomly distributed relative to folate deprivation predictions.
**Status:** **NOT MET but not strongly confirmed.** 58% match rate is above chance (50%) but not overwhelming. The mismatches are partially explainable by compensatory responses (IDH3 subunit stoichiometry, GOT1/GOT2 compartment switching).

### Kill Criterion 5: No Fortification-Incidence Correlation
**If:** Fortified vs non-fortified country comparison shows absolutely no signal.
**Status:** Awaiting Phase 3 epidemiological analysis.

### Kill Criterion 6: U-Shaped Curve Disappears with MTHFR Stratification
**If:** The reported U-shaped folate/ASD curve shows no interaction with MTHFR genotype.
**Status:** This specific analysis has not been published — novel prediction.

## 4. Key Insight

The hypothesis makes predictions at multiple levels:
- **Molecular** (gene expression patterns) — partially confirmed
- **Biochemical** (binding competition) — computationally plausible
- **Cellular** (CDR activation) — ENTPD6 signal consistent
- **Developmental** (window vulnerability) — model identifies 6-18 months
- **Epidemiological** (population patterns) — awaiting analysis
- **Therapeutic** (leucovorin response) — existing data suggestive

No kill criteria have been definitively met. The strongest evidence is the MAT2B finding and the coherent Cluster 21 signature. The weakest link is the 58% direction match rate in the broader mitochondrial set and the absence of direct folate transport gene disruption.
