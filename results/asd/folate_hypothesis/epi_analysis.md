# Phase 3: Epidemiological Analysis Report

**Research hypothesis under evaluation:** In MTHFR variant carriers, synthetic folic acid leads to UMFA accumulation, FRα blockade, reduced folate delivery to neurons, CDR activation, and ASD.

---

## 3a. Fortification Timeline Analysis

### Key Findings

**Fortification dates:**
- US: January 1, 1998 (140 µg FA/100g enriched grain)
- Canada: November 1, 1998
- Non-fortified: Germany, France, Norway, Sweden, Finland, Netherlands, Denmark, Israel

**ASD prevalence trajectories:**
- **US:** 1 in 150 (2000) → 1 in 68 (2010) → 1 in 36 (2020) → 1 in 31 (2024). Steep rise began late 1990s, has not plateaued.
- **Europe (non-fortified countries):** Pooled prevalence ~0.73%, compared to North America at ~1.01%. European estimates range 0.38-1.55% depending on country and methodology.

**Critical finding from MTHFR meta-analysis (Pu et al., 2013; Li et al., 2020, BMC Pediatrics):**
When stratified by national fortification policy, the MTHFR C677T-ASD association was significant **ONLY in countries WITHOUT mandatory fortification**. In non-fortified countries, ORs ranged from 1.95-2.78. In fortified countries, no significant association was detected. This suggests fortification masks the MTHFR-ASD link by providing enough folate to compensate for reduced enzyme activity — but does NOT address the UMFA/FRα blockade arm of the hypothesis.

### Confounders (CRITICAL)
- DSM-IV (1994) to DSM-5 (2013) broadened diagnostic criteria substantially
- Increased awareness, screening infrastructure, school-based identification
- European countries lack centralized autism surveillance comparable to CDC's ADDM
- Multiple concurrent environmental changes
- Direct US-Europe prevalence comparisons are methodologically unreliable

### Assessment: **AMBIGUOUS**

## 3c. Dose-Response / U-Shaped Curve

### Key Studies

**Raghavan et al., 2018 (Boston Birth Cohort, n=1,257)**
- Clear U-shaped relationship: Low intake (≤2x/week) HR 3.4; High (>5x/week) HR 2.3
- Highest folate decile (≥60.3 nmol/L): HR 2.5
- **Both folate AND B12 in top decile: HR 17.6 (95% CI: 6.5-28.9)** — striking synergy
- **MTHFR stratification performed: NO relationship found between maternal MTHFR C677T genotype, plasma folate levels, and ASD risk**

**Schmidt et al., 2012 (CHARGE study, n=429 ASD cases)**
- Periconceptional folic acid ≥600 µg was PROTECTIVE (aOR: 0.62)
- **Protection was STRONGEST in mothers and children with MTHFR 677 C>T variant genotypes**
- Gene-nutrient interaction: no prenatal vitamins + maternal MTHFR 677TT = combined OR 4.5

### Critical Nuance
Raghavan explicitly tested MTHFR stratification and found **NO interaction**. The CHARGE data shows MTHFR variants make FA **MORE protective**, not less. The U-shaped risk may operate through a different mechanism than MTHFR-mediated UMFA accumulation.

### Assessment: **PARTIALLY CONSISTENT, with significant caveats**

## 3d. Leucovorin (Folinic Acid) Trials

### Key Studies

**Frye et al., 2018 (RCT, double-blind, placebo-controlled)**
- Verbal communication improved by 5.7 standardized points (Cohen's d=0.70)
- **FRAA-positive subgroup: 7.3 points improvement (d=0.91, large effect)**

**Frye et al., 2021 systematic review (PMC8622150)**
- FRAA prevalence in ASD: **71% (64-77%)**
- ASD children 19.03× more likely to be FRAA-positive vs controls
- CFD prevalence in ASD: 38%
- Leucovorin response rates: overall 67%

**No MTHFR genotyping in any leucovorin trial.**

### Assessment: **CONSISTENT (with important gap)**

## Overall Synthesis

| Domain | Verdict | Key Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 3a. Fortification timeline | **AMBIGUOUS** | Higher US vs Europe rates exist but confounded. MTHFR-ASD link disappears in fortified countries (cuts both ways). |
| 3b. MTHFR × ethnicity | **INCONSISTENT** | No clean population-level correlation. Diagnostic bias dominates. |
| 3c. U-shaped dose-response | **PARTIALLY CONSISTENT** | U-curve real. But Raghavan found NO MTHFR interaction; CHARGE found MTHFR makes FA MORE protective. |
| 3d. Leucovorin trials | **CONSISTENT (with gap)** | CFD is real, treatable, FRAA-mediated in most cases. No MTHFR stratification. |

### What the data supports:
1. Cerebral folate deficiency is real in ~38% of ASD cases, primarily FRAA-driven (71% prevalence)
2. U-shaped folate dose-response exists — extreme high maternal folate doubles ASD risk
3. Leucovorin (bypasses the pathway) improves ASD symptoms, especially in FRAA+ individuals
4. UMFA does compete with methylfolate for FRα binding (established biochemistry)

### Where the hypothesis has problems:
1. **Raghavan 2018 directly tested MTHFR within the U-shaped model: NO genotype interaction**
2. **CHARGE data: MTHFR variants make FA MORE protective, opposite to hypothesis**
3. Population-level MTHFR-ASD ethnic correlations don't hold
4. FRAA mechanism (autoimmune) may be largely independent of MTHFR status
5. No leucovorin trial stratified by MTHFR genotype

### Refined interpretation:
The data is more consistent with: **Excess synthetic folic acid (regardless of MTHFR status) → UMFA → FRα competition + immune dysregulation → FRAA generation → cerebral folate deficiency → ASD risk.** The MTHFR component may contribute to individual vulnerability but does not appear to be the primary driver. The autoimmune (FRAA) pathway appears more central than the genetic (MTHFR) pathway.
