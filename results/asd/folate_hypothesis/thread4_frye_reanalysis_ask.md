# Thread 4: Actionable Ask — Leucovorin Trial MTHFR Reanalysis

**Date:** 2026-04-17
**Status:** FLAGGED — requires external collaboration

---

## The Question

**Does leucovorin response in ASD differ by MTHFR C677T genotype?**

This is the single most direct test of whether MTHFR status matters for the therapeutic arm of the folate-ASD connection. If MTHFR TT carriers respond better to leucovorin (which bypasses MTHFR), it would support MTHFR as a vulnerability modifier. If response is genotype-independent, it confirms the autoimmune (FRAA) pathway as primary.

---

## What Exists

### Frye et al. 2018 Leucovorin RCT
- Double-blind, placebo-controlled
- High-dose folinic acid (leucovorin, 2 mg/kg/day) in ASD children
- Verbal communication: +5.7 points (Cohen's d=0.70)
- FRAA-positive subgroup: +7.3 points (d=0.91)
- **MTHFR genotyping: Almost certainly performed** (standard in folate research) but NOT reported in stratified analysis

### Frye et al. 2021 Systematic Review (PMC8622150)
- Pooled FRAA prevalence: 71% in ASD
- Leucovorin response: 67% overall
- **No MTHFR stratification in any included study**

### Binding FRAA Biomarker Study (PMC10820361, n=110)
- 65% binding FRAA-positive
- FRAA titers predicted response on 5/6 SRS subscales
- **No MTHFR genotyping performed or reported**

## The Specific Ask

For Frye's group (or via Naviaux as intermediary):

> "In your leucovorin trial datasets, do you have MTHFR C677T genotyping data on participants? If so, a stratified reanalysis of leucovorin response by MTHFR genotype (CC vs CT vs TT) would directly test whether MTHFR status modulates treatment response independently of FRAA status. This would help distinguish between two competing models:
>
> 1. **MTHFR-dependent model:** TT carriers respond better because leucovorin bypasses their impaired enzyme
> 2. **FRAA-dependent model:** Response is genotype-independent, driven entirely by FRAA status
>
> A 2×2 analysis (FRAA+/- × MTHFR TT/non-TT) would be ideal."

---

## Why This Matters

| Outcome | Interpretation |
|---------|---------------|
| MTHFR TT responds better, independent of FRAA | MTHFR IS a primary driver — original hypothesis gains support |
| Response tracks FRAA only, MTHFR irrelevant | FRAA/autoimmune pathway is primary — revised hypothesis confirmed |
| MTHFR TT + FRAA+ has best response | Both pathways contribute — additive model |
| No difference anywhere | Neither pathway explains therapeutic variation — mechanism is elsewhere |
